# EverGesture MCP

EverGesture is a remote Model Context Protocol server for thoughtful gifting and life-occasion planning. It helps personal AI agents organize people and occasions, compare gifts or flowers, prepare reminders and delivery plans, and record approval before any external commitment.

## Connect

- MCP endpoint: `https://occalyn-mcp-gateway.navureli.workers.dev/mcp`
- Transport: Streamable HTTP
- Authentication: OAuth 2.1 with dynamic client registration
- Product: [evergesture.com](https://www.evergesture.com)

EverGesture never purchases, sends, publishes, or contacts anyone without a separate explicit approval. Its fulfillment approvals are exact-detail-bound, single-use, and expire after 15 minutes.

## What it can do

- Track confirmed people, occasions, preferences, reminders, and gift history.
- Research and compare gifts or flowers using recipient fit, occasion fit, delivered cost, delivery confidence, seller reliability, policy terms, and freshness.
- Prepare concise briefs, living cards, and approval-ready delivery plans.
- Support growth planning without publishing, contacting people, or spending money without approval.
